# Credits

OpenPlaceRules is published under the name “The OpenPlaceRules contributors”.

People who contribute code, data, translations or other work are listed here only if they opt in. We will not add a name, handle or link without that request.

To be listed, open a pull request that adds your preferred credit (a name or handle, and an optional link) under **Contributors who have opted in** below, or email hello@openplacerules.org with the wording you want.
