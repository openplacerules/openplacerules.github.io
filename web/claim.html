OpenPlaceRules

Claim your place

Run a venue? Publish your own rules and get the owner-verified badge. Owner-verified rules replace anything reported about your place, and AI agents and devices that query the registry see your rules first.

  1. Write /.well-known/place-rules.json on your website (spec; use the checker below).
  2. Start a claim to get a token, then prove you control the domain with one of: the token in that file, a meta tag, or a DNS TXT record.
  3. Press verify. We read your rules from the file and link them to your place.
