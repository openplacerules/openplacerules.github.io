# Data attribution

`data/seed.json` is licensed under the Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL), see `licenses/ODbL-1.0.txt`.

- **Boundaries and geocoded points:** © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), retrieved via the Nominatim API. Polygons are simplified.
- **Rules:** each entry is a short factual summary written for this registry, with a link to the public
  source it was taken from (`source.url`, `source.publisher`, `source.type`). Entries marked
  `secondary` rely on a news or aggregator page rather than the rule-maker's own publication.
  No source text is redistributed; check the linked source before relying on an entry.
- **Owner-verified entries** come from the owner's own `/.well-known/place-rules.json` and stay
  the owner's statement of their rules.
